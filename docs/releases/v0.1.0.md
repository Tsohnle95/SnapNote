### Meet your little screenshot companion

- **Grab the moment.** Press **⌘ B**, then select the part of your screen you want to keep.
- **Point it out.** Add arrows, boxes, or freehand marks. Undo and redo as you go.
- **Add your two cents.** Keep a written note alongside the screenshot, or copy the image reference and message together.
- **Give big captures more room.** The annotation area grows with your screenshot, and Move mode lets you drag from anywhere on the image.
- **Keep it yours.** Captures and notes stay in a folder on your Mac. No background uploads or telemetry.

### Install

1. Download and open **SnapNote-macOS-arm64.dmg**.
2. Drag **SnapNote** into **Applications**, then launch it from there.
3. Allow **Screen Recording** in **System Settings → Privacy & Security** when prompted. Quit and relaunch if macOS asks.
4. Find SnapNote in your menu bar, or press **⌘ B** to take your first capture.

> **First launch:** This release is ad-hoc signed and is not Apple-notarized. If macOS blocks it, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** after attempting to launch, then select **Open Anyway** for SnapNote. Only approve the app downloaded from this release.

### A couple of handy shortcuts

| Action | Shortcut |
| --- | --- |
| Capture / save and close | **⌘ B** |
| Copy the screenshot reference and context | **⌘ C** |
| Undo / redo annotations | **⌘ Z** / **⌘ ⇧ Z** when not typing |
| Save and close the popup | **Escape** or the green checkmark |

Captures save to `~/screenshots/snapshot/` by default. Change the location or capture shortcut in Settings. The red × discards the unsaved capture.
